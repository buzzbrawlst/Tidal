01 / WHO WE ARE

Introducing Tidal.

Tidal is a project built around the idea of bringing people together through software, community and shared experiences. It aims to create a straightforward place to discover updates, access project resources and stay connected.

The project is continuously being developed. New ideas, improvements and features are part of its journey, with community feedback helping shape what comes next.

Whether you're discovering Tidal for the first time or have been following its development, you're part of the experience.

02 / WHAT MATTERS

The ideas behind Tidal.

The project is guided by a few simple principles that influence how it is developed and where it goes next.

01 — COMMUNITY

People first.

A project should be shaped by the people who use it. Feedback and community involvement help guide development.

02 — DEVELOPMENT

Keep improving.

Development is an ongoing process. Ideas can change, features can improve and there is always room to build further.

03 — EXPERIENCE

Make it accessible.

Clear navigation, useful information and a consistent experience should make the project easy to explore.
